The Character Charter sheet

Every hero in psyblr is two things stacked together. One is a story anyone can write: a name, a look, skill names, a vibe. The other is a set of numbers nobody gets to write, because the engine owns them and the balance gate signs off on them.

We built it that way on purpose. This sheet walks through a finished hero with every field marked by who controls it, so if you're designing heroes, writing prompts for the director or deciding what I should make the forge generate next, this is the map.

The rule underneath all of it

You can call your ultimate anything, and it can look like anything you can describe, but the damage it does, the distance it reaches and the time until you can cast it again all come from a catalogue of 111 mechanics we built and priced by hand.

Your words pick a mechanic. The mechanic brings its numbers along.

I think of that split as the whole trick. It's what lets a stranger's hero land in the global loadout without breaking everyone else's game, and it's why the Spirit Bomb you describe will look like a Spirit Bomb while hitting exactly as hard as every other ultimate in its tier.

Kit (six slots, every hero)

FieldWhoWhat it is
Skill names and flavour textGeneratedYours. "Full Stack Launch" sits on top of the mechanic's own name.
Which mechanic fills each slotChosenThe matcher reads your skill description and picks the closest template from the role's catalogue by its tags (fire, beam, kamehameha and so on). You can swap to another template the role allows.
Damage, healing, shields, stuns, slows, knockbackEngineBaked into the template. Each slot type has a power budget (basic 70, active 22, passive 20, ultimate 14, movement 12) and every template is priced to fit it within 5%.
CooldownsEngineFrom the template, then cut by your cooldown stat up to a 40% ceiling.
Hit geometryEngineRange, radius, projectile speed and size, melee arc and beam width. Defaults are 30 m/s, a 0.3 m shot, a 90° swing and a 1 m beam, and anything bigger costs budget.
Passive triggerEngineAlways on, on hit, every third hit, below 35% health, or on a kill. It comes with the template.
TransformationEngineA few ultimates resize the hero for a while, like Titan Form going colossal for 8 s. Which size and for how long is the template's call.
How the AI uses itEngineEach template carries a usage hint (poke, engage, finisher, escape, peel, sustain, support) and a priority, so AI heroes play it sensibly.

Stats

FieldWhoWhat it is
Point spreadChosen30 points across Strength, Agility and Intellect, each capped per role (a mage can put 22 in Intellect, a tank only 16).
What a point buysEngineStrength gives health, armor, regen and a little weapon damage. Agility gives speed, attack and fire rate, weapon damage, lifesteal and ammo. Intellect gives spirit power, cooldown cut, magic resist and regen. The role's weights scale all of it.
The twelve secondary statsEngineHealth, armor, magic resist, move speed, attack speed, weapon damage, fire rate, ammo, spirit power, regen, lifesteal and cooldown cut. Each is derived, then capped per role.
Flight and jumpingEngineEvery hero flies. Flight is 1.3× ground speed, and jump range is 1.5 m per point of move speed.

The checks a hero has to pass

The balance gate

Every saved hero fights the six default heroes 30 times each on the real 60 Hz engine. A hero that wins more than 52% of those fights gets quietly retuned, up to six steps of 6% each, and if it still sits above the 55% ceiling after that, it's blocked, with the gate naming the slot that's doing it and the swaps that would fix it.

Army rules

Three of a role is fine. But the fourth copy and every one after it loses 10% of its power, down to a floor of 45%, while a side that fields four or more different roles gets +6% damage across the board, which is the "Mixed army" line you see light up in the staging panel.

What this means for the forge

The forge can get as creative as it likes with anything marked generated. It just can't invent a number.

If a player asks for something no template covers, like a time loop that rewinds health, our honest answer is a new template in the catalogue, priced and gated like the other 111. I'd rather add that template than pretend a clever prompt can get you there.
